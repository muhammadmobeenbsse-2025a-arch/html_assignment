The Alchemist

by Paulo Coelho

★★★★★ (5/5)

The Summary

A beautiful story about a shepherd boy named Santiago who travels from Spain to Egypt in search of a treasure buried near the Pyramids. Along the way, he meets a king, an Englishman, and an alchemist, all while learning to listen to his heart and follow his "Personal Legend."

My Verdict

"A must-read for anyone who feels lost or is searching for their purpose in life. The prose is simple but the wisdom is deep. It’s a book you’ll want to highlight on every page."
