Starry Night

By Vincent van Gogh

The Starry Night (1889) is an iconic oil-on-canvas painting by Vincent van Gogh, created during his stay at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence. A famous oil-on-canvas painting depicting the view from the asylum at Saint-Rémy-de-Provence.

The Persistence of Memory

By Salvador Dalí

The Persistence of Memory (1931) is a 9.5x13-inch Surrealist oil painting by Salvador Dalí, housed in MoMA, NYC. It features melting clocks, or "soft watches" to symbolize the flexibility of time and subjectivity of memory, famously inspired by melting Camembert cheese. The dreamlike, desert landscape, created using his "paranoiac-critical method" challenges linear reality.

Girl with a Pearl Earring

By Johannes Vermeer

Girl with a Pearl Earring (Dutch: Meisje met de parel)[1][2] is an oil painting by Dutch Golden Age painter Johannes Vermeer, dated c. 1665. Going by various names over the centuries, it acquired its present title towards the end of the 20th century.[3] The work has been in the collection of the Mauritshuis in The Hague since 1902 and has been the subject of various literary and cinematic treatments.
