Pakistani Special 🇵🇰

Authentic Chicken Karahi

⏱ 45 Mins 🔥 Medium Heat 👥 Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1kg Chicken (Karahi cut)
  • 5 Large Tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Ginger-Garlic paste
  • Green Chilies (sliced lengthwise)
  • 1 cup Yogurt (whisked)
  • Fresh Coriander and Ginger julienne for garnish
  • Spices: Crushed Black Pepper, Salt, and Cumin seeds.

Cooking Method

  1. Heat oil in a wok (Karahi) and fry the chicken until it changes color.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for 2 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes and salt. Cover and cook on medium heat until tomatoes are soft.
  4. Remove cover, mash the tomatoes into a paste, and add red chili flakes and cumin.
  5. Add yogurt and cook on high heat until the oil separates (Bhunayi).
  6. Garnish with lots of fresh coriander, ginger, and green chilies.
